# NutriAI - Comprehensive User Guide
Welcome to **NutriAI**, your personal AI-powered nutritional assistant! This guide will explain exactly what the app does, how the Artificial Intelligence calculates your meals, and how to use every feature to achieve your health goals.

---

## 1. Introduction: What Does NutriAI Do?
NutriAI is a smart recommendation engine designed to take the guesswork out of meal planning. Instead of just giving you generic meals, NutriAI:
1. Calculates exactly how many calories, proteins, carbohydrates, and fats your body needs based on your physical profile and fitness goals.
2. Scans through a massive database of foods.
3. Automatically **removes** any foods that violate your dietary restrictions or medical conditions (e.g., hiding all bread if you are Celiac).
4. **Scores and ranks** the remaining safe foods based on how perfectly they fit into your daily macronutrient targets.

---

## 2. Navigating the App

### The Landing Page
When you first open the app, you will land on the Home Page. Here, you can read a brief overview of the app's capabilities. 
*   **To begin:** Simply click the vibrant green **"Start Your Journey"** button to enter the main Dashboard.

### The Main Dashboard
The Dashboard is where the magic happens. It is divided into two main areas:
1.  **The Sidebar (Left/Slide-out):** This is your **User Context Panel** where you input all your physical details and dietary rules.
2.  **The Main Stage (Right/Center):** This is where your AI-calculated results will magically appear in beautiful, easy-to-read cards.

---

## 3. Configuring Your Profile (The Sidebar)
To get the best recommendations, you need to tell the AI exactly what you need. Open the sidebar (if you are on mobile, click the "Profile" button at the top) and fill out the following sections:

### A. Your Physical Profile & Goals
*   **Age, Height (cm), Weight (kg), Gender:** The AI uses these standard metrics to calculate your Base Metabolic Rate (how much energy your body burns just by existing).
*   **Activity Level:** Ranging from Sedentary (office job) to Extremely Active (athlete). This tells the AI how many extra calories to give you to fuel your day.
*   **Your Goal:** Do you want to **Lose Weight**, **Maintain Weight**, or **Build Muscle**? Selecting a goal dynamically changes the macro ratios the AI looks for. (For example, building muscle forces the AI to hunt for high-protein foods).

### B. Dietary Conditions (Strict Rules)
This is the most important safety feature of the app. 
If you check any of these boxes, the AI applies a **Strict Safety Filter**:
*   **Vegan / Vegetarian:** The AI will instantly lock out all meat, poultry, fish, dairy, and eggs.
*   **Celiac Disease:** The AI will instantly remove all gluten, bread, pasta, and wheat-based products.
*   **Diabetic / Hypertensive:** The AI will filter out ultra-processed foods and high-sugar/high-sodium items.
*   *Note: These rules are absolute. The AI will never recommend a food that violates a condition you check here.*

### C. Specific Preferences (Keywords)
Do you have specific cravings or dislikes? You can type keywords here separated by commas.
*   *Example 1:* If you type `"high protein"`, the AI will boost the score of protein-heavy foods.
*   *Example 2:* If you type `"no nuts"`, the AI will actively avoid recommending peanut butter or almonds.

### Generating Your Results
Once your profile is set, click the big **"Get AI Recommendations"** button at the bottom of the sidebar. The system will process thousands of calculations in milliseconds!

---

## 4. Understanding Your Results

Once the AI finishes thinking, your recommendations will populate the main screen.

### The "Top Picks" Podium
If you are on the first page of your results, the **top 3 foods** that most perfectly match your macro needs will be highlighted in a special "Top Picks" row at the very top.

### The Recommendation Cards
Each food recommendation is displayed on a beautiful card containing:
1.  **Match Percentage:** A score (e.g., `92% Match`) showing how perfectly this food aligns with what your body needs today.
2.  **Food Name:** The specific ingredient or meal.
3.  **Macro Snapshot:** The exact Calories, Protein, Carbs, and Fats you will get from a serving.
4.  **The AI Explanation:** A brief sentence explaining *why* the AI chose this for you. (e.g., *"This is an excellent lean protein source that perfectly aligns with your muscle-building goals without exceeding your fat limits."*)

### Pagination Navigation
Because the AI might find dozens of foods that fit your profile, the app uses pages to keep things organized. Use the **Previous** and **Next** buttons at the bottom of the screen to browse through your full list of matches (12 items per page).

---

## 5. Exporting Your Report
At the top right of the Dashboard, you will find an **Export Control Panel**. This allows you to instantly download your personalized AI recommendations to save for later or share with a nutritionist. 

You have three options:
*   **PDF:** Generates a beautifully formatted, multi-page presentation document containing your user profile, macros, and a neat table of all the foods.
*   **CSV:** Downloads a spreadsheet file. You can open this in Microsoft Excel or Google Sheets to sort or manipulate the data yourself.
*   **TXT:** Downloads a fast, lightweight text file containing a simple summary of your results.

*Note: All exports are generated securely inside your own browser to protect your privacy!*
